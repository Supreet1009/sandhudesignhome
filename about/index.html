About SandhuDesignHome

Thoughtful design. Honest building.

We combine practical residential construction experience with considered home design to create homes that feel personal, functional and built to last.

Our story

Built around the way people live.

SandhuDesignHome was established in 2022 with a clear goal: to make well-designed residential homes more accessible to everyday families.

Led by director Sukhwinder Sandhu, the business brings together more than five years of building and design experience, with a practical understanding of both construction and residential design.

Sukhwinder is a licensed builder and holds a TAFE qualification in design and architecture, giving SandhuDesignHome a strong foundation across both the creative and technical sides of home building.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, design thoughtfully, build properly and stay flexible throughout the process.

What matters to us

Value without compromise.

Affordable Pricing

We focus on realistic, considered solutions that help clients achieve a quality home without unnecessary cost or complexity.

Flexibility

Every family, site and budget is different. We work with our clients to create solutions that suit their needs rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all approach.

Quality Workmanship

From structural work to finishing details, quality remains central to how we design, manage and deliver each residential project.

Experience & credentials

A builder with a design mindset.

Having experience across both building and design allows SandhuDesignHome to consider more than how a home looks. We think about how it will be built, how it will function and how the overall project can remain practical for the client.

This integrated perspective helps create smoother communication from early design discussions through construction and handover.
