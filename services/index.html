Our Services

Design it well. Build it right.

From early home design through to complete residential construction, SandhuDesignHome helps clients move from idea to finished home with a practical and personal approach.

What we do

Residential homes made around you.

Every new home starts with a different block, budget, family and vision. Our services are designed to stay flexible around those differences rather than force every client into the same solution.

SandhuDesignHome focuses on two core areas: residential home construction and home design. Together, they create a smoother path from first ideas through to final handover.

01 · New Residential Home Builds

Quality construction from the ground up.

We build new residential homes with a strong focus on quality workmanship, practical value and clear communication throughout the project.

From site preparation and structural works through to interior finishes and final handover, our aim is to deliver a home that feels well considered at every stage.

Whether you're building your first home, a custom family home or a long-term residence, we work to balance your priorities with realistic construction decisions.

02 · Home Design

Designed around your lifestyle.

Good home design should do more than look appealing. It should work for your family, suit your block and remain realistic within your overall building budget.

Our design approach considers room layout, natural movement through the home, family needs, functionality and the relationship between design decisions and construction.

With building and design experience under one roof, we can approach design with an understanding of how those decisions translate into the actual build.
